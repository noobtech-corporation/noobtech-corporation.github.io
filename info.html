About Us

Our Mandate

The Noobtech Corporation is a government-funded organization responsible for the research and containment of anomalous phenomena. Unlike organizations built around destruction, our mandate prioritizes study, understanding, and safe long-term containment.

We operate a network of research and containment sites staffed by research personnel, field engineers, and specialized response units.

“We operate in darkness so humanity can do the same in the light.”

Values (placeholder): Containment over destruction. Precision over speed. Discretion in all things.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Frequently asked questions about Noobtech Corporation.

Is the Noobtech Corporation a real company?

Yes. The Noobtech Corporation is a government-funded organization currently in operation. Further operational details are not disclosed to the public.

What is a Hoverblock?

Hoverblocks are a proprietary hovering block platform developed by our engineering division. Additional technical specifications are not publicly disclosed.

What are the FRF and FEU?

The FRF and FEU are specialized internal divisions. Further information about these units, including what the names stand for, is not available to the public.

How do I apply for a job?

Select any "Apply for a Job" button on this site. It links directly to our Discord server, where applications are handled.

Do I need prior experience to apply?

We hire only the most qualified candidates for each role. Requirements vary by division — see the Careers page for details.
