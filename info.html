About Us

Our Mandate

The Noobtech Corporation was officially founded on August 16, 1977, shortly after the WOW! Signal was observed. The U.S. Government approached our founder because of his public fascination with the abnormal and funded the organization to investigate it.

Noobtech operates one major site hidden within Pictured Rocks National Lakeshore. Research, containment, and destruction work is carried out across the site’s six floors and its anomaly sectors.

“We operate in darkness so humanity can do the same in the light.”

Site rule: Clearance is required for every floor. If you see a sign leading to Sector Gamma, do not follow it.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Frequently asked questions about Noobtech Corporation.

Where is the Noobtech site?

The single major site is hidden within Pictured Rocks National Lakeshore. Employees must show their Noobtech ID badge to the monitored entrance camera to gain entry.

What is Sector Gamma?

Sector Gamma is not an official sector. Do not follow signs leading there or enter the bright-green door. The route leads to a version of the site with no functioning lights and severely accelerated biological processes.

How is the site organized?

Floor -1 contains sleeping quarters, bathrooms, and the armory. Floor 1 contains the cafeteria, gym, and recreation area. Floor 2 contains manufacturing and engineering bays. Floors 3 through 6 contain the anomaly sectors: Alpha, Beta, Delta, Epsilon, Zeta, Eta, Theta, Iota, Kappa, Lambda, and Delpha.

What clearance do I need?

Every floor requires clearance. General clearance covers floors -1 and 1, Engineering covers floor 2, Scientist 1 covers floors 3 and 4, Scientist 2 covers floor 5, and Scientist 3 covers floor 6. Some sectors may require more.

How are Noobtech technologies named?

Every Noobtech technology receives an acronym and an Mk. version number. For example, Self Driving Car becomes SDC Mk1.
